Market Traction

# The six stages of market traction

From a working product to segment dominance, there are predictable milestones on the way. Each has its own marketing and sales requirements.

1. [Eureka](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/eureka/): Stage one of market traction: your product is working and ready for customers. Next comes first revenue.
2. [Kaa Ching](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/kaa-ching/): Stage two of market traction: first revenue. Next comes building reference accounts your target buyers recognize.
3. [Two Thumbs Up](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/two-thumbs-up/): Stage three of market traction: credible reference accounts. Next comes choosing a beachhead segment and building momentum.
4. [Momentum](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/momentum/): Stage four of market traction: the whole company is focused on one segment with a predictable pipeline. Next comes building buzz.
5. [Building Buzz](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/building-buzz/): Stage five of market traction: you are a recognized key player in your target segment. Next comes segment dominance.
6. [Market Traction](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/market-traction/): Stage six: market traction. Your company dominates its segment and can afford to expand into the next one.

Stage one of six

## [Eureka](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/eureka/)

### It’s exciting to see your idea manifested in a working product!

From the beginning, you’ve envisioned the finished product and how it would work. Now it’s really here and ready for the customers.

### What’s next?

Focus will now shift to making the first sales and preparing to ship, install, invoice, and receive payment. In other words, to make your cash register ring — Kaa Ching!

Stage two of six

## [Kaa Ching](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/kaa-ching/)

### First revenue — that’s great!

To get that first revenue is such a satisfying feeling. Sure, it’s probably from another small local company that you have ties with, but it’s money in the bank.

### What’s next?

The next challenge is to develop reference accounts that are recognizable to the companies you are targeting. Reference companies have the key problem your product is designed to address and will serve as credible references to other buyers. This phase is called Two Thumbs Up.

Stage three of six

## [Two Thumbs Up](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/two-thumbs-up/)

### You closed some key accounts — amazing!

The fact that these companies have purchased and are happy tells prospects that your product works and you can deliver.

### What’s next?

Now, you’ve got to leverage this credibility to build momentum. You must evaluate the various market segments you are currently selling into, select the one in which you will build a beachhead, and then focus the company on this segment.

Stage four of six

## [Momentum](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/momentum/)

### The entire company is focused on dominating the selected market segment.

You have a sales pipeline and are getting better at identifying, selling, and closing sales with the target buyers.

### What’s next?

The next challenge is to build a broader awareness, and elevate your visibility so that the sale begins before your reps contact the target buyer.

[Read more: how to choose your beachhead segment](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/#momentum)

Stage five of six

## [Building Buzz](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/building-buzz/)

### Congrats — you’re a key player in your target segment!

This means your sales cycle should be shortening, since the target buyer knows who you are before your sales rep contacts them. It’s also known that your product is worthy of consideration by a target buyer — even before they are ready to buy. Your next challenge is to dominate the segment and reach the Market Traction milestone.

Stage six of six

## [Market Traction](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/market-traction/)

### You’ve done it!

Your company has dominated the selected market segment. Prospective buyers in this segment automatically look to your company for a solution. The stamp of credibility of the press and analysts helped fill your sales pipeline and made you attractive to strategic partners.
