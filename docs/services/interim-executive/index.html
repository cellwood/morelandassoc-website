Interim Executive

Bring in the seasoned C-level, marketing, commercial, and operations executive talent you need on an interim basis.

About interim executive services

Sourcing the right talent for your team is vital to your company’s success. Hiring the wrong leader can be both an expensive and disruptive mistake. Moreland’s interim executive service provides you a seasoned executive immediately.

Interim roles

Our seasoned executives have 20+ years of experience and step into these interim roles:

  • Interim Chief Marketing Officer (CMO): own the market with an AI-first go-to-market: positioning, messaging, awareness and the engine that turns it into pipeline, built for how buyers now find and evaluate you, including AI search and chat assistants. Connect what the market is telling you to the product team, and what you build to customers and investors.
  • Interim Chief Operating Officer (COO): turn a promising company into a well-run one with an AI-first operating model: the cadence, processes, tools and team to scale without breaking what already works.
  • Interim Chief Commercial Officer (CCO): lead the whole commercial engine, from marketing through sales, onboarding and customer success, with AI-first commercial operations, so early wins become repeatable revenue.

If necessary, we can field an entire team.

Why interim, not fractional

Roles like CMO, COO and CCO sit at the centre of the business: information flows in from the market, out from product and engineering, and up to the board and investors. Part-time leadership in these seats slows everyone down. Fractional works well for finance or IT. For the roles that run the business, you need someone in the seat.

Our interim executives have experience in everything from venture-funded technology start-ups to large enterprises. Our forte is parachuting into an assignment, seamlessly becoming a member of the team and making an immediate impact on the needs of the company.

As entrepreneurs, we think first about the business and what it needs to achieve to hit the next milestone. Our recommendations and actions are always in support of achieving the company’s success and building market traction.

We’re biased to action and believe in quickly getting the game in play; working hand-in-hand with sales; spending shrewdly to produce results; and providing transparency, metrics and persistent value.

We work closely with the executive team to identify the ideal permanent candidate for the position and to ensure a seamless transition once the person is hired.

When to use an interim CxO

Companies elect to use an interim executive in situations like these:

  • The company closed funding and has imminent go-to-market needs, but lacks a key executive on the team.
  • The company wants to make sure it hires the right person – not the first qualified person it interviews.
  • The company has parted ways with an executive.
  • The company has pivoted to a new market segment or business model and needs the right person to build traction in the new segment.
  • The company wants or needs to enter a new market and doesn’t want to distract the existing leadership from executing in current markets.
  • The company’s go-to-market needs rebuilding, AI-first, for how buyers now discover and evaluate vendors.

When big market shifts occur, such as a global economic downturn or the implosion of a market sector, the need to find a new successful market is imperative. An interim executive is a great option for quickly bringing in the seasoned management talent you need until you are ready to add to your permanent staff.
