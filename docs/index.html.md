Every company’s road to market traction runs through a series of milestones. When the next one calls for a different leader than the one you have, or one you don’t have yet, we step in. ### Why interim, not fractional

Roles like CMO, COO and CCO sit at the centre of the business: information flows in from the market, out from product and engineering, and up to the board and investors. Part-time leadership in these seats slows everyone down. Fractional works well for finance or IT. For the roles that run the business, you need someone in the seat.

### When to bring in an interim executive

- You’re pivoting or reaching a milestone, and the leader you need next is different from the one you have.
- You’ve chosen your beachhead segment and need someone to lead now and help you hire the right permanent person.
- Your go-to-market needs rebuilding, AI-first, for how buyers now discover and evaluate vendors.
- You’re not sure yet what the role should look like.
- You need help now, not after a six-month search.

## Market Traction

Market traction is having a dominant position in a market segment. It’s also called a beachhead. Driving to this point requires passion, agility, focus, commitment, and vision. In a word, entrepreneurship.

1. [Eureka](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/eureka/): Stage one of market traction: your product is working and ready for customers. Next comes first revenue.
2. [Kaa Ching](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/kaa-ching/): Stage two of market traction: first revenue. Next comes building reference accounts your target buyers recognize.
3. [Two Thumbs Up](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/two-thumbs-up/): Stage three of market traction: credible reference accounts. Next comes choosing a beachhead segment and building momentum.
4. [Momentum](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/momentum/): Stage four of market traction: the whole company is focused on one segment with a predictable pipeline. Next comes building buzz.
5. [Building Buzz](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/building-buzz/): Stage five of market traction: you are a recognized key player in your target segment. Next comes segment dominance.
6. [Market Traction](https://www.morelandassoc.com/market-traction/stages/market-traction/): Stage six: market traction. Your company dominates its segment and can afford to expand into the next one.
