# Insights

Straight answers to the questions founders, CEOs, boards and investors ask us about interim executive leadership.

## [Interim CMO vs fractional CMO: which does your company need?](https://www.morelandassoc.com/insights/interim-vs-fractional-cmo/)

If marketing needs to be led, not just managed, you need someone in the seat.

## [How long does an interim executive engagement last?](https://www.morelandassoc.com/insights/how-long-interim-executive-engagement/)

Typically four to nine months. The milestone sets the length, not the calendar.

## [When should a start-up hire an interim COO?](https://www.morelandassoc.com/insights/when-to-hire-interim-coo/)

When the company has outgrown the way it runs, and the permanent role isn’t clear yet.

## [What does a Chief Commercial Officer do, and when do you need one?](https://www.morelandassoc.com/insights/what-does-a-cco-do/)

A CCO runs marketing, sales and customer success as one revenue engine.

## [What is an AI-first go-to-market?](https://www.morelandassoc.com/insights/ai-first-go-to-market/)

Buyers now ask AI assistants before they ask your sales team. What changes, and what doesn’t.
