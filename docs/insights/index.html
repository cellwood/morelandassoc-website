Insights

Straight answers to the questions founders, CEOs, boards and investors ask us about interim executive leadership.

Interim CMO vs fractional CMO: which does your company need?

Marketing work is cross-functional and time-sensitive. Consistent availability, smooth responsive operations, and coordinated work need full-time executive leadership.

How long does an interim executive engagement last?

Typically four to nine months. The milestone sets the length, not the calendar.

When should a start-up hire an interim COO?

When the company has outgrown the way it runs, and the permanent role isn’t clear yet.

What does a Chief Commercial Officer do, and when do you need one?

A CCO runs marketing, sales and customer success as one revenue engine.

What is an AI-first go-to-market?

Buyers now ask AI assistants before they ask your sales team. What changes, and what doesn’t.
